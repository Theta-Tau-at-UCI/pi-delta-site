## Identity

- **Organization:** Theta Tau — the nation's oldest and largest professional engineering fraternity.
- **Chapter:** Pi Delta Chapter, University of California, Irvine.
- **Founded at UCI:** Winter 2011 · **Installed as Pi Delta Chapter:** April 14, 2011.
- **Size:** Over 200 active members and alumni.
- **Region:** Western Region of Theta Tau.
- **Site name / alt:** "Theta Tau: Pi Delta Chapter" / "Theta Tau UCI".

## Brand

- **Colors:** maroon `#4A0404` (and near-black) backgrounds; gold accent `#C9A84C`.
- **Type:** serif display (Fraunces) + sans body (Inter). Premium but approachable.
- **Aesthetic:** dark-primary UI (refs: Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Spline, acmeatuci.org).

## Pillars

- **Brotherhood** — a close, supportive network of engineers who grow together.
- **Professionalism** — "We help our brothers develop strong communication, problem-solving, and
  leadership skills... preparing each other for the professional world." (chapter copy)
- **Service** — "We are known for our service to our college, university and the larger community.
  Our service projects create a unifying environment for learning and personal growth." (official)

## Recruitment

- Rush is held **every fall and spring quarter**; meet brothers at the **Engineering Quad booth**.
- **Fall 2025 schedule:** 9/29 Info Night (Business Casual, open) · 9/30 E-Challenge (Casual, open) ·
  10/1 Pro-Dev Night (Professional, open) · 10/2 BBQ Night (Casual, invite only) · 10/3 Interview
  Day (Formal, invite only).
- Eligibility: engineering, CS, or data science major; 2.0 GPA minimum; ~$200 dues every two quarters.
- Full FAQ lives in `src/data/chapter.ts` (`recruitmentFaqs`).
